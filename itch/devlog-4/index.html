A new GRIP update is live!

Three big things this time: a brand new category, racing at night, and the world board on itch.io.

🏁 New Category: GT3

Ten modern GT cars join the grid, each with three liveries to choose from: thirty in total. GT3 races are GT3 only, so you fight cars of your own kind.

With 35 cars and 14 circuits, there are now 490 trophies to collect.

🌙 Racing at Night

Pick Day or Night before the start, in solo and in online lobbies. At night the track goes dark: floodlights pick out the gravel traps, headlights cut through the corners, and brake lights glow red when the pack slows down.

GT, GT3 and prototypes have headlights, each car with its own shape. The old F1 cars have none: they rely on the circuit lights. The cars and the lap times don't change, only what you can see.

🌍 World Records on itch.io

The world board now works right here on itch.io. The best time of every car is shown without an account. Sign in with Google (it opens in a new tab) and your best laps go on the board, just like on the web version.

🔧 And Also

  • A crisp beep now marks every lap you complete.
  • Kitsune Zeta 240 and Purosangue Freccia: redrawn rear fenders on all their liveries.
  • The title screen links to the latest devlog.

🚦 What's Next?

I'm still refining the cars, the circuits and the driving. Tell me how night racing feels, and which GT3 is your favourite!

Play the latest version here: https://grip-game.github.io/

Feedback, suggestions and bug reports are always welcome!
